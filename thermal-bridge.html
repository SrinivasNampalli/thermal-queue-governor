A consistency pass is a limited result.

It means the held-out fourth state agrees with the guarded model and assumed calibration. A heat path that bypasses the guard can give a false cool estimate while passing. A shared calibration error can also escape. Neither accuracy nor safe motor operation is established.

A concept experiment, not a motor test.

The hardware is illustrative. Curves and estimator outputs come from bundled synthetic cases. The model prescribes guard temperatures; it does not size a heater/cooler or include its energy. Physical development needs calibrated heat paths, guard dynamics, and selected-motor tests. No physical validation or patentability conclusion is implied.
