Declared measurement assumptions

Decision details

“Within limit” is a model classification at an illustrative threshold. It grants no permission to operate a motor.

The unresolved boundary

A different heat path can still hide heat.

The revision identifies a bypass ending on the reference that is actually moved. A separate path to an unchanged ambient boundary is indistinguishable from a cooler source in the stipulated model. The evidence retains a 130°C source estimated as 85°C, with a conditional interval that misses the truth.

Independent numerical challenge

Finite response. Three additional outcomes.

A separate coupled differential-equation plant adds source and pad thermal mass and finite guard/reference actuator response. The estimator sees measured endpoint channels. Numerical convergence and energy conservation check the integration; they do not validate a physical motor.

Evidence and next step

Physical validation is still a future experiment.

A bench protocol specifies an independent source thermometer, actual path-interception checks, wider-range sensor comparisons, actuator energy measurements and acceptance criteria frozen before physical data collection. No equipment or measured calibration has been supplied. Patentability also remains unresolved.
