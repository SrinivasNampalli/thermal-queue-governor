01 / The problem & the approach

Heat keeps building after the sensor stops reading.

01

A reading has a ceiling.

A clipped sensor cannot reveal how far the actual temperature has risen. TQG keeps a possible temperature interval instead of treating the clipped value as truth.

02

Accepted commands carry heat.

Commands already in the first-in, first-out queue still have to execute. Their future thermal effect belongs in today’s decision.

03

Admission starts with headroom.

The governor forecasts the upper bound, accounts for the queue, and admits the effort that fits the model’s remaining thermal budget.

02 / How it works

Every new command
gets a thermal forecast.

The upper temperature bound and accepted queue travel through every admission decision.

  1. 01 / OBSERVE

    Temperature

    Read the sensor. Recognize when it clips.

  2. 02 / ESTIMATE

    Bounds check

    Update the possible temperature range.

  3. 03 / FORECAST

    Command queue

    Account for effort already accepted.

  4. 04 / ALLOCATE

    Thermal budget

    Calculate the remaining headroom.

  5. 05 / DECIDE

    Admit or reject

    Admit full or reduced effort when it fits. Reject if none fits.

The original governor reduces effort to fit. The prototype also offers a full-command waiting mode with a 20-second expiry.

Research scope, assumptions & limitations

The 1,120-episode total combines 900 main-study episodes, 100 actuator-reporting follow-up episodes, and 120 clipping-ablation episodes. It includes several controllers and deliberate assumption violations. The zero-breach and 90.4% effort figures above apply specifically to 120 primary valid-model TQG episodes.

These are finite synthetic simulations under declared model and actuator assumptions. In a separate inaccurate-actuator-reporting study, the estimated interval missed the true temperature for 113 samples while its validity flag remained on. The model does not authenticate physical actuation, and no hardware safety guarantee is established.

The primary TQG peak was 104.9999719°C, only 0.0000281°C below the 105°C limit: a numerical boundary result, not a validated physical safety margin. The bootstrap resamples 20 paired seeds with all six scenarios retained; it does not estimate field failure risk. The repository also retains a separate 900-episode pilot (2,020 completed episodes in all); that pilot is excluded from the 1,120 reported-study total.

The full-command waiting and expiry scheduler is a demonstration extension. It does not add research episodes or alter the results reported in the manuscript.
